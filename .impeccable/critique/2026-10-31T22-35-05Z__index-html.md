## Overall impression

Credible, useful, and technically grounded, but it asks newcomers to understand the product's categories before showing them a first success. The biggest opportunity is a direct path from “What is this?” to “I can run and inspect a program.”

## What's working

- The product explanation names concrete computers and actual programming workflows rather than relying on vague claims.
- Four labeled entry cards make screenshots, downloads, user guides, and developer guides discoverable; four choices are not inherently excessive.
- Real screenshots, explanatory captions, and development-versus-release caveats build trust.

## Priority issues

1. **[P1] Ordinary links lose essential visibility and focus cues.** In [css/style.scss](/home/vbmacher/projects/emustudio/emustudio.github.io/css/style.scss:32), all links lose outlines and underlines. Soft Coral against white calculates to **2.51:1** contrast; Signal Coral headings against the pale panel calculate to **2.86:1**. These are calculations from declared colors, not sampled browser pixels. The [viewport declaration](/home/vbmacher/projects/emustudio/emustudio.github.io/_includes/header.html:7) also sets `maximum-scale=1.0`; browser enforcement varies. This makes reading and keyboard use harder, particularly for learners with low vision. **Fix:** retain coral identity with darker reading-link variants, restore visible keyboard focus and inline-link differentiation, and remove the zoom restriction. **Command:** `$impeccable harden`.

2. **[P2] The opening has no clear recommended start.** [index.html](/home/vbmacher/projects/emustudio/emustudio.github.io/index.html:14) leads with platform/framework distinctions and Follow/Sponsor controls. Getting started appears later, with four equally styled actions. Download is available in navigation, so this is a hierarchy problem rather than a blocked task. **Fix:** give the opening a concise learning outcome, a prominent Download action, and one real application screenshot; keep contribution links secondary. Exact above-the-fold composition needs browser verification. **Command:** `$impeccable layout`.

3. **[P2] The novice handoff repeats the introduction.** The [user-documentation card](/home/vbmacher/projects/emustudio/emustudio.github.io/index.html:98) opens [another platform/framework introduction](/home/vbmacher/projects/emustudio/emustudio.github.io/_documentation/user/_doc/introduction/index.md:15), rather than beginning an exercise. Students and teachers must assemble their next steps themselves. **Fix:** recommend one existing example and connect installation, opening the computer, compiling, running, and inspection into a first-experiment path. Retain the full reference guides. **Command:** `$impeccable onboard`.

4. **[P2] Screenshot sizing lacks responsive constraints.** [Thumbnails](/home/vbmacher/projects/emustudio/emustudio.github.io/_includes/screenshots.html:9) have a fixed 300px width, and [modal images](/home/vbmacher/projects/emustudio/emustudio.github.io/_includes/screenshots.html:27) lack a container-width constraint. Overflow is a source-supported risk, not a reproduced browser failure. **Fix:** constrain both image variants to available width, retain aspect ratio, and verify narrow screens and zoom. **Command:** `$impeccable adapt`.

## Cognitive load and emotional journey

**Moderate: 3/8 checklist failures** — single focus, hierarchy, and progressive disclosure. Chunking, grouping, sequential decisions, choice count, and memory demands are broadly reasonable. Six navigation entries alone do not establish overload.

Curiosity meets a credible technical explanation, then loses momentum while the visitor translates terminology and selects a route. Screenshots make the experience concrete. The novice documentation handoff returns to explanation instead of delivering the peak of a first working program.

## Persona red flags

- **Sam, keyboard or low-vision visitor:** ordinary inline links lack a custom replacement for suppressed focus outlines and have low declared-color contrast.
- **Casey, mobile visitor:** fixed thumbnail widths and unconstrained full-size images risk awkward viewing; mobile composition remains unverified.
